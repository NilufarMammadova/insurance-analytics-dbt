## Insurance Policy Status

Defines the current status of an insurance policy.

| Status    | Description                    |
|-----------|--------------------------------|
| ACTIVE    | The policy is active.          |
| EXPIRED   | The policy has expired.        |
| CANCELLED | The policy has been cancelled. |
